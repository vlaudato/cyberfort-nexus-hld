# 2.4 Performance & Capacity Requirements

Performance defines the system's responsiveness and throughput under load.
Capacity defines the resources required to operate within each sizing tier.

### Throughput

- **NFR-004**: The system MUST sustain the following peak IP traffic ingestion rates without packet loss:

  | Tier | Peak throughput |
  |------|----------------|
  | Small | 1 Gbps |
  | Medium | 5 Gbps |
  | XLarge | 50 Gbps |

### Response Time

- **NFR-005**: The InvP MUST load dashboards within 5 seconds under normal operating conditions.
- **NFR-006**: The InvP MUST return search query results within 10 seconds for queries spanning up to 30 days of data.
- **NFR-007**: Alerts generated by the FA and C&C engines MUST be visible in the InvP within 60 seconds of detection.

### Storage

- **NFR-008**: The system MUST provision sufficient storage for NF metadata retention (3 months), FA file/email retention (3 months), FA scan logs retention (1 year), and C&C log retention (1 year) per sizing tier. *[AS-008, AS-009, AS-010, AS-011, AS-012]*

  | Tier | NF (3 months) | FA Files (3 months) | FA Logs (1 year) | C&C Logs (1 year) | Total |
  |------|---------------|---------------------|------------------|-------------------|-------|
  | Small | 260 GB | ~1.4 GB | ~3 GB | ~0 GB | ~264 GB |
  | Medium | 1.3 TB | ~7.2 GB | ~15 GB | ~0 GB | ~1.32 TB |
  | XLarge | 13 TB | ~72 GB | ~146 GB | ~0 GB | ~13.2 TB |

  See [Sizing Derivations](#sizing-derivations) for the full calculation behind these figures.

### Concurrent Users

- **NFR-009**: The InvP MUST NOT alow more than 50 concurrent active users.

## Sizing Derivations

> Preliminary capacity derivations. Full analysis planned for MS3.

### NF Storage (3-month retention)

**Inputs:**
- 100 IP flows/min/endpoint on average *[AS-008]*
- ~100 bytes per netflow record (5-tuple + timestamp + storage overhead) *[AS-009]*
- 3-month retention (90 days) *[FR-NF-004]*

**Calculation:**

```
Records/day  = endpoints × 100 flows/min × 60 min × 24 hours
Storage/day  = records/day × 100 bytes
Storage (3m) = storage/day × 90 days
```

| Tier | Endpoints | Flows/day | Storage/day | Storage (3 months) |
|------|-----------|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| Small | 200 | 28,800,000 | 2.88 GB | **~260 GB** |
| Medium | 1,000 | 144,000,000 | 14.4 GB | **~1.3 TB** |
| XLarge | 10,000 | 1,440,000,000 | 144 GB | **~13 TB** |

NF metadata is the dominant storage consumer across all tiers.

---

### FA Infected File Storage (3-month retention)

**Inputs:**
- ~40 emails/endpoint/day (industry average)
- ~0.1% malware infection rate *[AS-010, Verizon DBIR]*
- ~2 MB average malicious payload *[AS-010]*
- FTP file transfer volume considered negligible in modern enterprise networks *[AS-010]*
- 3-month retention (90 days) *[FR-FA-008]*

**Calculation:**

```
Emails/day        = endpoints × 40
Malicious/day     = emails/day × 0.001
Storage/day       = malicious/day × 2 MB
Storage (3m)      = storage/day × 90 days
```

| Tier | Endpoints | Emails/day | Malicious/day | Storage/day | Storage (3 months) |
|------|-----------|------------|---------------|-------------|-------------------|
| Small | 200 | 8,000 | 8 | 16 MB | **~1.4 GB** |
| Medium | 1,000 | 40,000 | 40 | 80 MB | **~7.2 GB** |
| XLarge | 10,000 | 400,000 | 400 | 800 MB | **~72 GB** |

FA infected file storage is negligible relative to NF (~1% at XLarge).

---

### FA Scan Logs (1-year retention)

**Inputs:**
- Every inbound file/email is scanned, not just malicious ones *[FR-FA-003]*
- ~1 KB per scan log entry (AV scan response JSON + storage overhead) *[AS-011]*
- 1-year retention (365 days) *[FR-FA-009]*

**Calculation:**

```
Scans/day    = endpoints × 40 emails/day  (all emails scanned)
Storage/day  = scans/day × 1 KB
Storage (1y) = storage/day × 365 days
```

| Tier | Endpoints | Scans/day | Storage/day | Storage (1 year) |
|------|-----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| Small | 200 | 8,000 | 8 MB | **~3 GB** |
| Medium | 1,000 | 40,000 | 40 MB | **~15 GB** |
| XLarge | 10,000 | 400,000 | 400 MB | **~146 GB** |

---

### C&C Log Storage (1-year retention)

**Inputs:**
- Only suspected C&C activity (above threshold) is logged *[AS-012, FR-CC-004]*
- C&C detection rate estimated at ~0.0002% of outbound flows *[AS-013]*
- ~200 bytes per C&C log entry (flow metadata + confidence score + verdict)
- 1-year retention (365 days) *[FR-CC-005]*

**Detection rate derivation** — see [AS-013](README.md):

```
CyberFort supports max 50 SOC analysts [NFR-009]
Industry average: ~174 alerts/analyst/day (Vectra AI 2026)
CyberFort's share of total SOC alert volume: ~15%
  (enterprise SOCs run 10–28 security tools)

Max C&C alerts/day = 50 analysts × 174 alerts/day × 15% = ~1,300 alerts/day

XLarge outbound flows/day:
  10,000 endpoints × 100 flows/min × 50% outbound × 60 min × 24 hours
  = ~720,000,000 flows/day

Detection rate = 1,300 / 720,000,000 ≈ 0.0002%
```

**Storage calculation:**

| Tier | Outbound flows/day | Detections/day | Storage/day | Storage (1 year) |
|------|-------------------|----------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Small | 14,400,000 | ~29 | ~6 KB | **~2 MB** |
| Medium | 72,000,000 | ~144 | ~29 KB | **~10 MB** |
| XLarge | 720,000,000 | ~1,300 | ~260 KB | **~95 MB** |

C&C log storage is negligible across all tiers. Logging all outbound flows rather than detections only would increase XLarge storage to ~52 TB/year *[AS-012]*.
