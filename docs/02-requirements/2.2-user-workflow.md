# 2.2 User Workflow Requirements

User workflow requirements describe the primary interactions a SOC analyst performs within the Investigation Portal (InvP). The corresponding component interactions are described in [3.2 Processes](../03-high-level-design/3.2-processes.md).

### Alert Triage

Related runtime sequences: [File Analysis](../03-high-level-design/3.2-processes.md#321-file-analysis), [Network Forensic Ingestion and Query](../03-high-level-design/3.2-processes.md#326-network-forensic-ingestion-and-query), and [Manual Investigation Creation](../03-high-level-design/3.2-processes.md#324-manual-investigation-creation).

A SOC analyst starts their day by reviewing the Investigation Portal dashboard, which displays all unassigned alerts and their open investigations. A new FA alert appears: a malicious PDF was detected in an inbound email addressed to endpoint 10.0.5.23. The analyst opens the alert to inspect the details: source engine, verdict, file metadata, and the AV scan report. After reviewing, they take ownership of the alert to signal to the rest of the team that this is being investigated. To understand the broader context, they query the NF data for all traffic to and from 10.0.5.23 over the past 24 hours, looking for unusual outbound connections that might indicate the endpoint is already compromised.

### ACI Investigation Review

Related runtime sequences: [Automatic Investigation Creation](../03-high-level-design/3.2-processes.md#323-automatic-investigation-creation) and [Review and Ownership of an Automatic Investigation](../03-high-level-design/3.2-processes.md#325-review-and-ownership-of-an-automatic-investigation).

Scenario 1: The ACI has already processed this alert and analyzed all traffic to and from 10.0.5.23 over the past 24 hours. It identified suspicious activity and created a new automatic investigation. While the analyst reviews the alert details, they notice that the ACI has already created the investigation. The analyst opens the ACI investigation, reviews the correlated timeline, and takes ownership but cannot modify the ACI-generated content.

### Manual Investigation

Related runtime sequences: [Network Forensic Ingestion and Query](../03-high-level-design/3.2-processes.md#326-network-forensic-ingestion-and-query) and [Manual Investigation Creation](../03-high-level-design/3.2-processes.md#324-manual-investigation-creation).

Scenario 2: The analyst sees a pattern in the NF data correlated to the alert that the ACI missed. The analyst creates their own investigation, links the FA alert, attaches the relevant NF data, and continues editing and refining their investigation as new data emerges.
