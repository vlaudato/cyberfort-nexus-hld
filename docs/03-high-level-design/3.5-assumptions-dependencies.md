
# 3.5 Assumptions & Dependencies

The complete set of design assumptions is maintained in the [requirements assumptions table](../02-requirements/README.md#assumptions). The assumptions with the greatest architectural impact are:

- The gateway switch can mirror all required traffic to a dedicated monitoring port (AS-001/002).
- POP3 and FTP are the relevant inbound protocols for payload extraction (AS-005).
- Traffic volumes, record sizes, and retention projections are representative enough for deployment sizing (AS-008 through AS-013).
- The supplied C&C model and the 0.70 detection threshold are suitable, pending validation with production traffic (AS-003/006).

CyberFort Nexus also depends on services and infrastructure outside its control:

- The customer gateway switch and its monitoring port provide the traffic feed.
- A pre-trained C&C model is supplied; model training and selection are out of scope (AS-003).
- The 3rd-party Antivirus provides a deployable REST API and the required licensing (AS-004).
- The customer Identity Provider provides user authentication when available; local accounts provide the required fallback (NFR-014).
- The customer provides sufficient on-premises compute, storage, and network capacity for the selected sizing tier (NFR-008/010/021).
